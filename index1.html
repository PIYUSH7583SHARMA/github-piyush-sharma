Welcome to Maharashtra

Maharashtra is a beautiful state in India. It is famous for its forts, beaches, mountains, cities, historical monuments and rich culture.

About Maharashtra

Maharashtra is one of the major states of India. It is famous for its historical forts, cities, festivals, food and natural beauty.

Culture and Heritage

Maharashtra has a rich cultural heritage. Marathi language, traditional music, dance, festivals and historical monuments are important parts of its culture.

Popular Places in Maharashtra

Mumbai

Mumbai is the capital city of Maharashtra and is famous for the Gateway of India, Marine Drive and Bollywood.

Pune

Pune is known for its educational institutions, historical sites and cultural heritage.

Lonavala

Lonavala is a beautiful hill station famous for its greenery, waterfalls and pleasant weather.

Mahabaleshwar

Mahabaleshwar is a popular hill station known for its mountains, viewpoints, strawberries and waterfalls.

Historical Forts

Maharashtra is famous for many historical forts such as Raigad, Sinhagad, Pratapgad and Shivneri.
